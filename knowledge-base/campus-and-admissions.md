## 2. Advanced Cyber Lab Infrastructure & Cyber Range
Codevirus Academy houses high-performance practical laboratories designed to replicate tier-1 Security Operations Centers (SOC) and offensive red-team environments:

- **Dedicated Enterprise SOC Simulation Cyber Range**:
  - Multi-node lab running live Wazuh SIEM, Splunk Enterprise, Suricata IDS, Zeek network monitor, and TheHive incident response platform.
  - Real-time attack-and-defense simulations where students analyze active malware, APT traffic, and network intrusions.
- **Offensive Security & Hardware Lab**:
  - Specialized penetration testing equipment: Hak5 WiFi Pineapple, Flipper Zero, Proxmark3 RFID/NFC cloner, Alfa AWUS036ACM wireless adapters for packet injection, and RTL-SDR radio analyzers.
- **High-Performance Compute & Cracking Clusters**:
  - Workstations equipped with NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPUs for Hashcat / John the Ripper hash cracking, offline rainbow table lookups, and AI model security testing.
  - Gigabit redundant low-latency leased line fiber internet with dedicated static IPs.
- **Virtualization & Cloud Sandbox**:
  - Dedicated Proxmox / ESXi virtualization servers providing each student with isolated Kali Linux, Commando VM, FlareVM, and vulnerable target instances (Metasploitable, Active Directory labs, OWASP Juice Shop).

## 4. Admission Process, Eligibility & Flexible EMI Options
- **Eligibility Criteria**:
  - Open to students and graduates from B.Tech (CS/IT/ECE/EE), BCA, MCA, B.Sc (IT/CS), Diploma holders, working professionals, and career switchers.
  - No mandatory prior coding or programming experience required; networking and Linux fundamentals are taught from absolute ground zero.
- **Application & Onboarding Steps**:
  1. **Free Counseling & Lab Demo**: Schedule an in-person or live virtual demo with senior faculty.
  2. **Technical Aptitude & Goal Assessment**: Align interests (SOC Blue Team vs Bug Bounty / Pentesting vs Cloud Security).
  3. **Batch Allocation**: Select weekday or weekend batch preference.
  4. **Document Verification**: Government ID (Aadhaar / Passport) and academic marksheets.
- **Fee Payment Structures & Financial Support**:
  - Flexible monthly installment (EMI) plans with 0% interest financing options.
  - Early-bird and merit scholarships (up to 20% fee waiver for academic distinction or women in cybersecurity).
  - Corporate-sponsored training vouchers accepted.
